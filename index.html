Field guide

Basic safety instructions for common disasters.

🌊 Flood

Move to higher ground. Avoid walking or driving through moving water.

🌋 Earthquake

Drop, cover and hold. Stay away from windows and damaged buildings.

⛰️ Landslide

Move away from unstable slopes and follow official evacuation instructions.

🔥 Fire

Leave the building using stairs and contact emergency services when safe.
