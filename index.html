Field guide

Simple safety instructions during common disasters.

Move to higher ground. Avoid moving water. Disconnect electricity if safe. Keep emergency supplies ready.
Drop, cover and hold. Stay away from windows. Do not use elevators. Move to an open area after shaking stops.
Move away from unstable slopes. Avoid valleys during heavy rain. Report cracks or slope movement.
Stay low in smoke. Use stairs. Never use elevators. Call emergency services when safe.
Stay indoors away from windows. Secure loose objects. Keep water, food and a torch ready.
